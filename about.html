I started in print, designing magazines, identities, and ads. I learned to solve problems on a grid before I ever touched a screen. That discipline carried me into UX, then product design leadership, then AI-assisted products.

Today I design software for hard problems: nuclear scheduling, pharma sales, payments, cybersecurity. I still sketch by hand before I open Figma.
