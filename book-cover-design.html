02

Why It Matters

Readers decide in a thumbnail. Most indie covers lose there.

A weak cover doesn't get a bad review. It gets scrolled past, and you never find out why sales stalled. Here's what I fix.

Genre signals
A cover has to say "this is your kind of book" before it says anything clever. I design to the shelf you're selling on.
Type that holds at 100px
Titles get tested at Amazon search size, not just full screen. If it can't be read small, it doesn't ship.
Print-ready, first time
Spine math, bleed, barcode space and CMYK handled to KDP and IngramSpark specs, so your proof isn't a surprise.
A real art director
You work directly with me: concept, type, image sourcing and production, without a template mill in between.

Series Design

One system. Every book instantly part of the set.

The Tracks trilogy by J. Gabriel Gates and Charlene Keel: three covers built on one framework, so readers who finish book one spot book two on sight.

Plan your series
Fixed framework
Same title lettering, series badge and author block in the same positions on every volume.
A color per volume
Blue, red and green tell the books apart at thumbnail size while keeping them a set.
One hero object
Each cover centers a single image from its story: the crown, the tunnel, the train.
Built to extend
Book four slots in with no redesign. You get the template and style guide.

Questions

Do I own the cover?

Yes. You get full commercial rights to the final design for your book in every format. Stock images are licensed in your name where the license allows.

How do payments work?

50% deposit to book your slot, 50% on approval of final files.

What do you need from me?

Title, author name, genre, a short synopsis, three to five comparable titles, and for print, your trim size and page count.

Do you use AI-generated art?

Rarely. Covers are built from licensed photography, illustration and original type work. If AI imagery is the right tool for a specific concept, I'll tell you up front and we'll decide together, so you always know what's on your cover and that it's cleared for commercial use.

Do you work with publishers?

Yes. Small and mid-size presses can book single titles or a monthly block at a per-title rate.
