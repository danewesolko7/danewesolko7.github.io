What I Bring

Things that have to be made, not just shown.

Print literacy

I build things that have to be made. A pocket mailer with slits for removable inserts, rebuilt from a physical proof, is the kind of job I like.

One system, many pieces

A logo is the start. At Spartico it ran through the website, menus, and flyers. At Copperline it ran through cards, letterhead, and envelopes. In the media kit, three brands ran as one.

Editorial range

Covers, features, directory inserts, and full issues. I've laid out entire magazines, not just single spreads.

Illustration with a point of view

An ASHPE Bronze for cover illustration, and a June 2015 cover that was meant to be fun in a serious issue.

Case in Depth — The Brazilian Court

The lost mailer.

The Brazilian Court's original files were gone and they needed a reprint.

Work like this

Start from the object
